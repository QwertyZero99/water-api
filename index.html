Did You Know?

The Safe Drinking Water Act was enacted in 1974. Since then, strict regulations have been put in place to ensure that America’s drinking water remains safe. Since then, while the laws still generally ensure safety, contaminants still may pose major problems for recreational bodies of water and potentially drinking water supplies.
