Did You Know?

The Safe Drinking Water Act was enacted in 1974. Since then, strict regulations have been put in place to ensure that America’s drinking water remains safe. While these laws still generally ensure safety, contaminants may still pose major problems for recreational bodies of water and potentially drinking water supplies.
